# _HIDDEN IMMUNITY IDOL INFO:_

Every season of Fiovivor will always have some sort of Immunity Idol, which will be hidden within the server. Could it be a just hidden within a simple role or reaction? Perhaps one of the hosts has a social media account linked to their profile that has something of value!

The general rule of thumb with my idols is that you will have to work for it, I will make you guys suffer through evil hunts, but they will always BEGIN with something in this server. Even if it’s something external, you’ll find that link through clicking around in this server.

When an Idol is flushed in any way, including when it is played, it will be re-hidden. I will not disclose if one has been secretly flushed (if someone is voted out with it in their pocket), but if one is played at Tribal Council, I will say something along the lines of “this will be re-hidden”. I may not re-hide Idols if it is too late in the game.

There will never be more than two normal Hidden Immunity Idols in play at one time. There could be two, plus maybe a TEMPORARY Idol in play, but never more than two regular Idols. You will not, under any circumstances, be Cirie’d out in one of my seasons.

# _[REHAB:](https://open.spotify.com/track/7wCmS9TTVUcIhRalDYFgPy?si=98f741adf4614caf)_
On top of giving us a challenge submission each round, you'll also have the option of giving us a Rehab Vote. This vote can be for ANYONE in the game, and it will be done privately in Submissions. Some of the losing Tribe(s) may have their most voted player sent to Rehab, effectively exiling them for the remainder of the round and allowing them to skip Tribal Council. In the event of a tie, a live Tiebreaker vote will be held via reactions. Additionally, you cannot attend Rehab consecutively. This means that if you're sent in Episode 4, you cannot be sent in Episode 5. If there is no Rehab during Episode 5, it carries over such that you wouldn't be able to go in Episode 6.
