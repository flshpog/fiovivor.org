Welcome to Sequester!
This specific game is exclusively played over Discord.
You MUST have a working camera and microphone! We want to see your pretty faces <3
This is moreso the fun aspect of it but if you have alcohol, weed, a pen, anything that you might enjoy, bring it along and let loose!

What is Sequester?
Sequester is a one night, social-strategic game where every elimination round, there is some sort of damning twist that you as a player must maneuver around. Each round will typically last around 10-20 minutes allowing you time to communicate effectively with the cast. In total, this game can take anywhere from 4-6 hours. Typically, it starts with 20 players, however, that can be changed to whatever number we got! Voting is always anonymous unless stated otherwise and self voting IS allowed unless said otherwise.

There are 4 main aspects of Sequester! Room limits, LOS’, Drag Round(s), and Jury.

What is a room limit / what are room limits?
It is a maximum a room can get to before someone MUST leave the call. It is something that is HEAVILY enforced. Once a room limit has been reached, a production member will join and yell until the room is under it. So, if the room limit is 5 for example, if there are 6 in the call, then the production member will yell the phrase “room limit” until someone leaves. These limits will continue to get smaller as the game progresses.

LOS’
This is a Lotus of Safety, think of it as Survivor’s hidden immunity idol. It is guaranteed safety for whoever it is played on. It saves them from being voted out or dragged! You must tell us in your submissions chat if you are using it. If not, it will go unplayed and it will not be our fault. It is NOT automatically played for you.
To find a LOS, you must use your detective skills and search in the server. It is a certain phrase or emoji you are looking for. It can be anywhere so keep your eyes out. THEY WILL ALWAYS EXPIRE AT F6

Drag Round(s)
These are rounds in which after a player is eliminated, they get to choose ONE person to come with them out of the game. This is normally and formally done at the F4, the 2 people left after the drag will be in the finale. However, there is a chance that drags are done even before the F4! The only way that dragging is prevented is if an LOS is played. This is the only time an LOS is eligible to be played post the vote counting period.

Jury
Following a certain point in the game, usually halfway through, players eliminated will become members of the Jury. The group of individuals here are sequestered from the spectators and will vote at the finale for who they believe deserves to win the game. It is advised and CRUCIAL that you stay during this period. Not only do you get other Juror’s inputs on the game, but at the end you get to ask your questions and say whatcha need to say to the F2. If for some reason you need to leave, we will ask you to inform us in your submissions and then submit a list of who you’d like to win. Highest being who you want to win and lowest being who you wouldn't want to win. This is a last resort, we hope and expect you'll stay to the end!

Other Rules;
No outside dming or communicating
This is a rule we will be a stickler on. If you are caught messaging, you will be removed or called out in front of the cast. DMing ruins the integrity of the game. Don’t do it.

No Pre-Gaming
Do not use prebuilt relationships to boost yourself in the game or previous games to target another player. It also ruins the integrity of the game. Please refrain as much as possible in doing so.

No bullying, harassment, or hate of any kind.
You can get dramatic, you can get heated but remember it is a game. We are all here to enjoy ourselves. Don’t force the hosts to step in and have to make a fool out of you.
