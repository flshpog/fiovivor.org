Credits

Fan-made 3D models used on the Everest Survivor S3 board, with thanks to their artists. Mario and everything in it belong to Nintendo; this is a non-commercial fan project.

  • Boos (the Underworld board): "Boo (Super Mario Bros)" by Yanez-Designs, Sketchfab, CC BY 4.0. Joined, centred and given a faint glow.
  • Small Yoshi eggs (Yoshi's Island board): "Yoshieggs (Super Mario Bros)" by Yanez-Designs, Sketchfab, CC BY 4.0. Split into single eggs, resized, smaller textures.
  • Apples (Yoshi's Island trees): "Super Mario - Berry" by kingston.shamily, Sketchfab, CC BY 4.0. Resized.
  • Watermelons (Yoshi's Island trees): from "Low Poly Fruit Pack" by RoVenRa, Sketchfab, CC BY 4.0. Taken out of the pack, stood upright, smaller texture.
  • Mushroom, Golden Mushroom, Slow Mushroom, Poison Mushroom: "Power Up Mushroom (Super Mario Bros)" by Yanez-Designs, Sketchfab, CC BY 4.0. Recoloured, crown added to the Golden Mushroom.
  • Blue Shell: "Blue Shell (Mario Kart)" by Yanez-Designs, Sketchfab, CC BY 4.0.
  • Bullet Bill: "Bullet Bill (Super Mario Bros)" by Yanez-Designs, Sketchfab, CC BY 4.0.
  • Custom Dice Block: "Mario Party Dice Block" by animatezach, Sketchfab, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Unaltered apart from size.
  • Magic Lamp: "Aladdin Lamp" by GOFOR3D, Sketchfab, CC BY 4.0. Simplified, new gold material.
  • Crystal Ball: from "Cutesy Styled Wizard Themed Props" by ttorion, Sketchfab, CC BY 4.0. Taken out of the set.
