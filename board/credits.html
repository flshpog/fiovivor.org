  • Custom Dice Block: "Mario Party Dice Block" by animatezach, Sketchfab, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Unaltered apart from size.
  • Magic Lamp: "Aladdin Lamp" by GOFOR3D, Sketchfab, CC BY 4.0. Simplified, new gold material.
  • Crystal Ball: from "Cutesy Styled Wizard Themed Props" by ttorion, Sketchfab, CC BY 4.0. Taken out of the set.
