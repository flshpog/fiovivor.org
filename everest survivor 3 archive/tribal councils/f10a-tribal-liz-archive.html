You may not give ponderosa/VIP spec info to people still in the game. Pre-Jurors, you will likely not be invited back for another season if you leave the server immediately after you’re voted out, it’s bad etiquette. Jurors, you’re not allowed to specify where your jury vote is going or you’ll be given a strike.
