How funny would it be to tell you all that when the first four players were voted out of the game, they were not actually eliminated, but rather sent to the Underworld and given a chance to return to the game? This twist is generally more well known as...

REDEMPTION ISLAND
A duel between all inhabitants of the Underworld will take place each round. (I say "duel" because most often it will be a 1v1 for survival.) The winner of the duel will stay alive and continue their quest to re-enter the game), and the loser becomes permanently eliminated.

All advantages in a player's inventory automatically expire upon being voted out. You will never be able to hold advantages in The Underworld. Upon returning, you will receive temporary immunity but production will never gift you an advantage in the game for returning nor will you get back the advantages that were once yours.

Because this twist was revealed late as part of the surprise behind it, I will throw you guys a bone in the form of transparency.
