EP. 2 IMMUNITY : "Codenames"

In this immunity challenge, your tribe will be paired up against another tribe at random in a game of [codenames!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Codenames_(board_game)) The rules to codenames are very simple. Each tribe will have one spymaster, while everyone else will be an operative.

Guide to Spymaster:
You will see the whole board, and which words belong to which team! At the start of your tribe's turn, you must give them a one-word hint and a number. The hint cannot contain ANY pre-existing words on the board, and whatever number you submit will be how many guesses (+1, there will always be one "extra guess" in a turn) the operatives can make that round.


Guide to Operative:
Some words belong to your team and some words belong to the opposing team. Based on the clues your spymaster has given you, you will guess words on the board that you think belong to your team. Get it right, and your turn keeps moving! Get it wrong and your turn ends.


In the attached visual example, you will see some words belonging to a red tribe and some words belonging to a blue tribe. The spymaster sees all. The blue tribe has an extra word in this instance because the tribe that goes first always has one extra word.
https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1414321682415357965/1560431637995585679/image.png?ex=6ad2b0b5&is=6ad15f35&hm=6d10318744cef09d44019d2ed722afd9d408eb6c82d82c9661a59f4c2db366d8&
The word penguin serves as the death word. Colored dark and dull, it does not belong to either team. Guessing the death word at any point will automatically lose your whole team the challenge.

When you have been paired against another tribe, a chat will open with all players from both tribes involved. The first player to volunteer for each tribe will become the spymaster of their respective tribe. From there, a turn order is determined and it's game-on!

Giving Hints
Spymasters, in your submissions you will enter a command such as !hint (word)-#, with (word) serving as the hint for your tribe # being... well, your number! So for example if I was hinting at my tribe to match "Cookie" and "Ice Cream", I could enter !hint desert-2 and hope they can connect the dots. Your hint must be a valid word from the [Scrabble dictionary](https://scrabble.merriam.com/) or else your hint is considered invalid and your tribe will automatically lose.


Guessing
Operatives, in the challenge chat you will enter your guess in a command such as !guess (word), with (word) serving as a placeholder for which word on the board you are attempting to get for your tribe. Your tribe can keep going if you get it right! But your turn will end if you guess a word meant for the other tribe or meant for neither tribe. Make sure you spell it right or the bot won't recognize your guess! Also, running commands anywhere outside of the designated area will have your tribe will automatically lose.
