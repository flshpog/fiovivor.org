Hey does anyone want an unsolicitated chili recipe
JOEL FIRECRACKER CHILI
1 pound ground beef
1 can kidney beans, 1 can of northern beans (drained) (16oz each)
1 can tomato paste (12oz)
1 can of roasted dice tomatoes (16 oz)
Full sweet onion (diced)
Garlic (minced) (1 tablespoon)
Two jalapeños (minced) (adjustable)
Red bell pepper (6oz, diced)
Orange bell pepper (6oz, diced)
2-3 haberno peppers (two minced, one for optional garnish) (adjustable)
Salt (2 tsp)
Pepper (1/2 tsp)
Paprika (1 tsp)
Oregano (1/2 tsp)
Garlic powder (1 tsp)
Crushed red pepper (1/4 tsp)
Cumin (1/4 tsp)
Cayanne (1/2 tsp)
Slap yo mama (1/2 tsp, 1 tbsp)
Water (1 cup)
Chicken bouillon cube (dissolved in water)

- Mix half salt, pepper, paprika, oregano, garlic powder, crushed red pepper, cumin, cayanne, 1/2 tsp slap yo mama in bowl
- Sautee beef until brown, add in spice mix
- Add to instant pot
- Sautee onion and garlic paste until onion is beginning to turn transparent in same pan as beef
- Add in bell peppers and habanero, sautee for 2-3 minutes longer until onion is carmelized
- Add to instant pot
- Add tomato paste, diced, beans, half salt, and 1 tbsp slap yo mama to instant pot
- Dissolve boullion cube in water and then add to pot
- Cook on pressurized for one hour
- If you’re gonna use an extra habanero as garnish, cut into slices on side
