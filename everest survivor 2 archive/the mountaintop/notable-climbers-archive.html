1. JAKEY
@Unknown User | 🥉🥈🥉
Jakey became the first player in Everest’s prequel series to become a multi-finalist! In @FIO5, he built the connections necessary to advance both him AND his Blood vs. Water partner to FTC, where Jakey finished 3rd and his duo 1st. He earned a very controversial PLAYER OF THE SEASON title for his performance, which was given to him for being able to dictate the momentum of the game while not having to get his hands dirty. Jakey returned the very next season for @FIO6, immediately emerging as a physical and social threat, but he took on a much more strategic game (on top of his challenge prowess and Idol hunting), staying loyal to a few key allies and systematically cutting people and slaying the season’s dragon at F4. The Jury was very mixed on Jakey’s game as he burned many of them, which led the Jury to crown the universally beloved Vini as the winner over Jakey and Gem by a vote of 6-4-0. But this isn’t the end of Jakey’s story. He returned once again 8 months later for @FIO8 as one of six returnees and found himself being targetted by Woofley, the eventual season winner early on, but after re-establishing a working relationship with his S6 ally Drew and a new ally in Jessica, the three of them formed the season’s most successful alliance and Jakey would make FTC for his third straight season, upholding his legacy of never being voted out. Along the way, he would also break the Immunity record at the time, winning six straight challenges from F8-F4. Jakey has averaged 2 Jury votes each finale across his 3 Fiovivor seasons (6 total) and won a total of 9 Individual Immunities.

2. xSOUL
@xSoul | 🥇🥇
xSoul began his journey being one of twenty newbies on the @FIO4 cast. With this being xSoul’s first ever longterm Survivor ORG, he had no idea what he was getting himself into and he had no idea the amount of time and energy he would need to invest to be successful… but xSoul did not do that for the first few rounds of the game. Instead, he took a backseat, which caused him to be lower on his trainwreck tribe’s social heiarchy. Fortunately for him, his #1 ally in Sadie stumbled her way onto Hog Mountain and discovered the Battle Healer shrine, which allowed Sadie to save her ally, negating five votes against him at a Tribal she wasn’t even at, allowing xSoul to advance in the game. This served as a wake-up call for xSoul that he had to be more present in the game, and so he did. He became a notorious player, only fueled by his challenge prowess and cocky demeanor. At the merge his target skyrocketed after coding a physical robot to loophole an app game challenge to grant him immunity, he assumed multiple rivalries and survived many close votes before being able to fully win out to FTC at the endgame, barely snagging a win over two strong competitors in a 4-3-1 vote.
He returned as a mentor for @FIO7 where he built a cross-tribe communication system for his Mentees that was technically legal under Fiovivor’s ruleset.. and he would push the boundaries once again upon his return to @FIO9 as one of six returning players and the only former winner on the cast. He and his girlfriend Katie instantaneously became the top dogs of the season, dominating the game despite being so hated and disliked by the revolting faction. Luckily, xSoul and Katie were able to dispose of Syn, their strongest vocal opposition at the swap by a vote of 5-4 after Syn got cocky and tried to hold onto their SW/OP. At the S9 merge, xSoul once again had a skyrocketing threat level after using the Alive Player VC to insert himself into the madness that was the F14 Wall Tribal, but survived without immunities for rounds that the cast decided to pick off inactive players instead of take a shot at the power structure. At the F10, the tides had changed and Katie would have been blindsided if not for xSoul pulling off the greatest idol play in series history, taking a HUGE risk to play the idol for his girlfriend and leave himself vulnerable, which worked in his favor and Faye went home, allowing the dominos to fall one by one, misting strong players like Mike and Riley into making foolish and reckless moves that allowed xSoul and Katie to both make F5 where they both found themselves vulnerable after Riley won a clutch immunity. The other three players decided to spare xSoul and eliminate Katie, but after he dominated the final immunity challenge, xSoul made his second FTC and easily won over another Jury by a vote of 6-2-2.

3. TIFF
@Tiff Tiff | 🥉🥉
Just like the man right before her, Tiff’s adventure begins in the @FIO4 universe; Tiff completely dominated the early social and strategic game, but her game quickly crumbled after the tribe was looking to vote out the one and only fortnitefred2005 from the game, causing Tiff to make the decision to MUTINY from her tribe to be with Emp, an ally she had briefly met and found herself working with long-term. The mutiny raised her threat level, causing her to almost get voted out at the swap, but she survived by a half-vote when she was able to eliminate her former ally Kenny by a vote of 2.5-2. After a hectic pre-merge, she had a lot of work to do at the merge to re-position herself, which she did. She was a part of Flawless’ idol-out & blindside, systematically sacrificed her allies and built new working relations with everyone on the cast. She voted out her biggest Jury threat at F6, convinced xSoul to play an idol on her at the F5, but when her #1 ally Emp experienced an unfortunate family emergency and had to withdraw from the game and the Jury, Tiff had no support from the Jury and finished 3rd by a vote of 4-3-1 despite playing the best game on paper. Tiff would return two years later for @FIO10 where she once again finished in 3rd, where her game was not nearly as strong as the first time around, but Tiff cemented her legacy as a double-finalist, the series’ best confessionalist and gagged the entire server when she flipped on Katie at the F6 revote.

4. CRISTEN
@Unknown User | 🥈🥈
Love her or hate her, she is without a doubt one of the most impactful people on the series. She served as Fio’s original co-host for Fiovivor 1, 2 and 3 before retiring. She made her grand return in @FIO7 where at her second Tribal in the game, she found herself in hot water after her friend Jiv, who served as one of the season’s Mentors, had informed his Mentees over Cristen’s social prowess. After Kyle swooped in to save the day by passing his idol along to Cristen, she was able to blindside power-player Claw by a vote of 8-2! At the swap, Cristen would build her strongest connection and working relationship with Quartz, where they worked together to then blindside Jessica by a vote of 4-3-1 with an idol in her pocket and flushing another idol from the game. At the merge, Cristen made her first big mistake in letting go of Sera, who was loyal to Cristen since the Claw vote. She and Quartz then BOTH lost their Blood vs. Water partners, and they then lost an ally in Henke after Cristen tried to warn him to play his idol but he did not listen. Cristen placed 2nd at the FTC by a vote of 5-2-1, where her Blood vs. Water partner did not vote her to win the game. Cristen then returned to serve as an Executive Producer for Fiovivor 8 and 9, and made her official return as an All-Star in @FIO10 where she once again played a very reckless strategic game with a complete chokehold on the social game. Her poor decision-making and Jury management won out over the genuine connections and relationships she formed throughout the season where she earned her second silver medal by a vote of 6-4-0.

5. WOOFLEY
@Woofley | 🥇🥇
Woofley, Woofley, Woofley. The second double winner of the prequel series and the yin to xSoul’s yang. If you had told me Woofley would emerge as a double winner at the start of @FIO8, you would’ve been seen as crazy, because Woofley’s start was not pretty. She attempted to take down Jakey at the first Tribal Council of the season which would eventually be cancelled due to a walk, causing a line in the sand to be drawn at the very first opportunity. Woofley’s tribe won out until the swap, where she then met Kebab, her saving grace. Kebab finally found the re-hidden idol with 15 minutes to spare at a Tribal where Woofley was going home, sniping a very bitter Derek V. from the game. On Halloween of 2025, Woofley lost Kebab at a Joint Tribal Council after Luis successfully played an Idol on Cameron, but Woofley kept fighting and got revenge for Kebab when she blindsided Cameron at the F12 by scooping up Bazz, Ore and Jessica. And after Kebab returned from the Distortion World and the game had officially merged for good, the #WoofleySweep was in motion until Bazz flipped on Ore and Kebab, and with Luis returning to the game it wasn’t looking good. However, Woofley, the cat with 9 lives, overcame the odds and survived the endgame completely vulnerable and managed to sit next to two players the Jury did not necessarily respect, and so they awarded her the win by a vote of 7-2-2.
Woofley then returns as a former winner heading into the All-Stars gauntlet of @FIO10, where in similar fashion to her first win, she found herself in a really bad spot early on, and at one point was going home in 24th place. She recovered, very well in fact. She allied up with big-name players like Tiff, Riley, Saturn, Cristen, and notoriously Stephen, whom she won a clutch live challenge with after the New Schoolers wanted to throw to eliminate Stephen from the game. But after Stephen had essentially eliminated himself during the “How Far Will You Go?” round and Riley was about to be picked off, Woofley’s game was beginning to sink as she was relentlessly bullied by Brent. Riley gave all of her Safari advantages to Woofley on her way out the door, which allowed Woofley to play a clutch SW/OP on herself following the Brent Tribal, where her threat level spiked after she disposed of her rival. She gave another one of her Riley-advantages to Virgo to use against Tiff, which fueled a season-long rivalry between the two of them allowing Woofley to further advance in the game. At the F6, revote, she convinces Tiff to flip on Katie when her neck was on the chopping block, setting the stage for her win out during the F5 and F4 rounds and barely snatch the crown over Cristen and Tiff by a Jury Vote of 6-4-0.
