Rules/Reminders:
- Trust Rankings: When you submit your vote at Tribal Council, we also need a list of everyone on your tribe, ranked by how much you trust them. We will remind you if you don't provide them, but if you refuse to provide them you will receive a strike.
- No parchment meta: You may not, at any point describe to someone what your parchment will look like or plan around whose parchment belongs to who. You also can’t do shit like, “let’s vote this person with blue text parchments and an image of their character!” either. Anything else is fair game.
- No “waiting until the last hour of Tribal to vote and strategize” meta: You may change your vote as many times as you want to until the deadline. If all the votes are in early, I will do a poll along the lines of “5 :thumbsup: reacts for an early Tribal”, and if the vote passes the votes will be read early.
- Idol Play Info: Pre-playing idols is 100% allowed if you cannot be there for the live vote reading. Additionally may play your advantage around a condition. For example, “I play my idol on myself if an idol is played on this person”. You can’t do stupid ass conditions like “I play my idol for my ally if they get the most votes” or anything like that.
- Final Words Info: In the event you are voted out of the game, you may pre-submit final words in your submissions, type them live after the vote reading, or in your submissions after. You may not give ponderosa/VIP spec info to people still in the game. Pre-Jurors, you will likely not be invited back for another season if you leave the server immediately after you’re voted out, it’s bad etiquette. Jurors, you’re not allowed to specify where your jury vote is going or you’ll be given a strike.
